PANDEMONIUM

— GOTTA COOK 'EM ALL —

For as long as anyone remembers, the world has been home to wild, living kitchenware — mischievous Appliances that roam the homes, markets, caves, and crystal dimensions of the realm.

Professor Bouillabaisse has given you one task: complete the PANDEX — a registry of every known Appliance. All 86 species. Common spatulas. Rare blenders. Mythic espresso machines. Legendary sous-vides. And three ULTRA entities whispered about only in chef's lore.

Catching an Appliance requires skill. Battle it, weaken it below 30% HP, then throw a Takeout Box. Rarer Appliances demand sharper technique — and the tall golden grass hides surprise encounters.

Battles drop 🧺 ingredients — bring them to Chef Gourmand at the Grande Mart to cook 🍳 dishes for powerful buffs and forge 🔪 garnishes your Appliances can equip. Earn 🪙 coins from every win, evolve your team, face your rival Marco, conquer the 🏯 Battle Tower, and once you've earned your 2nd star, seek the sealed 🔐 Pantry Vault. Press M any time you're lost — the world map always shows your next objective.
