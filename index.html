— GOTTA COOK 'EM ALL —

For as long as anyone remembers, the world has been home to wild, living kitchenware — mischievous Appliances that roam the homes, markets, caves, and crystal dimensions of the realm.

Professor Bouillabaisse has given you one task: complete the PANDEX — a registry of every known Appliance. All 41 species. Common spatulas. Rare blenders. Mythic espresso machines. Legendary sous-vides. And two ULTRA entities whispered about only in chef's lore.

Catching an Appliance requires skill. Time your strikes, hammer buttons, hold your nerve, follow precise sequences, and take careful aim. Rarer Appliances demand sharper technique.
